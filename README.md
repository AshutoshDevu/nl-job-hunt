# Netherlands Job Hunt

A personal tracker for software engineering roles in the Netherlands at **IND-recognised sponsors** (Highly Skilled Migrant route).

- `jobs.json`: roles collected from public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee) of companies on the [IND public register of recognised sponsors](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors/public-register-regular-labour-and-highly-skilled-migrants), filtered to NL tech roles that fit ~2 years of experience, plus hand-picked roles.
- Each role lists the skills from its description, which ones still need study, a short study roadmap, LinkedIn searches for recruiters and managers, and a follow-up email template.
- Status, notes and study progress are stored in your browser (localStorage). Use **Export** to back them up.
