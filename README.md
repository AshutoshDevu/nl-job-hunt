# Europe Job Hunt

A personal tracker for software engineering roles in the Netherlands (at **IND-recognised sponsors**, Highly Skilled Migrant route), Germany, Luxembourg and Switzerland.

- `jobs.json`: roles collected from public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee, Personio, SmartRecruiters) and public listings (LinkedIn, Arbeitnow, Y Combinator, Hacker News, Landing.jobs). Dutch roles are limited to the [IND public register of recognised sponsors](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors/public-register-regular-labour-and-highly-skilled-migrants). Filtered to tech roles that fit ~2 years of experience, plus hand-picked roles.
- Each role lists the skills from its description, which ones still need study, a short study roadmap, LinkedIn searches for recruiters and managers, and a follow-up email template.
- Status, notes and study progress are stored in your browser (localStorage). Use **Export** to back them up.
